# LeagueCred Distribution Roadmap

## Objective

Bring the first 1,000 people to LeagueCred organically and turn them into a durable football knowledge community.

The goal is not 1,000 empty registrations. The success target is:

- 1,000 registered members within 60–90 days
- At least 300 weekly active lockers
- At least 25 active lockers in every launch community
- A repeatable acquisition loop driven by members, supporter communities, and public records

## Core Positioning

LeagueCred is not another betting-tip feed. It is a free community where supporters make one high-confidence call in the team, region, or league they genuinely know, build a transparent public record, and combine their knowledge with other specialists.

Primary message:

> One team you truly know. One high-confidence call. A permanent public record. Completely free. Let's win together.

Supporting messages:

- This is a community for people who follow football closely, make selective calls, and want to win together.
- Do not click every possible game. Lock only the game you are almost certain about.
- Know your team. Represent your league. Let other supporters do the same for theirs.
- Every win and every miss remains visible. Credibility is earned, not claimed.
- LeagueCred is completely free.

"The bookmaker's nightmare" can be used as occasional campaign copy, but it should not be the main positioning. The main brand should emphasize supporter expertise, selectivity, transparency, and community—not paid tips or guaranteed gambling outcomes.

These numbers are operating targets, not guarantees. They make the required daily activity measurable.

## Distribution Principles

1. Density beats breadth. A league with 25 participating supporters is more valuable than hundreds of inactive accounts spread across many leagues.
2. Recruit contributors, not spectators. Seek people who will make a lock, explain it, return, and invite somebody else.
3. Start with identity. People join to represent a club, league, region, or supporter community—not merely to use another website.
4. Make results the marketing. Public wins, misses, streaks, and league standings are more credible than promotional claims.
5. Never hide losses. Transparent records are LeagueCred's primary differentiator.
6. Join existing conversations respectfully. Ask administrators for permission and provide value before sharing links.
7. Keep LeagueCred the primary community. External platforms should distribute and coordinate activity, not replace the product.
8. Remain free and avoid affiliates, deposits, paid tips, and promises of guaranteed outcomes.

## Launch Communities

Begin with eight focused club or league communities rather than trying to activate every league simultaneously.

Selection criteria:

- A passionate and active online supporter base
- Accessible administrators, creators, podcasts, or supporter groups
- Enough upcoming fixtures for a weekly habit
- At least one language in which the LeagueCred team can communicate authentically
- A realistic path to recruiting 5–10 credible founding members

For each launch community, recruit 5–10 Founding Captains from:

- Long-time supporters
- Fan-account operators
- Podcast hosts, guests, and active listeners
- Supporters' club members
- Thoughtful Reddit, Discord, Facebook, X, or forum contributors
- People who regularly provide useful match analysis

### Founding Captain Benefits

- A permanent Founding Captain badge
- A public performance profile
- Recognition on the relevant league or club page
- Early access to product improvements
- A personal invitation link
- The ability to host a weekly community challenge
- A direct feedback channel with the LeagueCred team

Status, ownership, and a credible public record should be the reward. Do not buy early participation with unrelated prizes.

## Founder-Led Recruitment

The first 100 members should be recruited personally. During the first month, direct conversations are more important than generic posting.

### Daily Outreach Target

- Identify 30–40 relevant people or community administrators
- Send personalized messages rather than bulk messages
- Record the community, contact, date, response, and resulting activation
- Follow up once when appropriate
- Stop when a person or community declines

### Supporter Outreach Script

> I'm building LeagueCred, a completely free football knowledge community. You make one high-confidence call in the team or league you genuinely follow, and your record stays transparent. I'm inviting ten founding supporters for [league/club]. Would you like one of the founding profiles?

### Community Administrator Script

> Could we run a free [community name] confidence challenge? Members submit one call, and we publish an honest weekly recap—including the misses. There are no paid tips, affiliate links, or deposits.

### Creator or Podcast Script

> Your audience already has deep knowledge of [club/league]. LeagueCred can host a free weekly challenge where each person makes only one high-confidence call and represents your community on a transparent leaderboard. We will create the recap and link back to your show/community. Would you like to test it for one matchweek?

### Outreach Rules

- Mention why the person or community is a good fit.
- Do not mass-DM or paste identical messages everywhere.
- Ask permission before posting promotional links.
- Participate genuinely in existing communities.
- Do not misrepresent LeagueCred as having more activity than it does.
- Do not manufacture testimonials, accounts, predictions, or records.

## The Weekly Community Ritual

LeagueCred needs a predictable event that members recognize and anticipate.

### Monday: Transparent Settlement

- Publish the previous matchweek's results.
- Include wins and misses.
- Recognize accurate communities and individual specialists.
- Invite members to discuss what they learned.

### Tuesday: Supporter Spotlight

- Feature one supporter and the club or league they know.
- Show their record and recent reasoning.
- Explain what first-hand knowledge they bring.

### Wednesday: New Challenge Opens

- Open the Community Lock challenge.
- Announce the current league-versus-league or club-versus-club matchup.
- Give every member one clear action: make this week's lock.

### Thursday–Friday: Insight and Recruitment

- Publish short supporter observations.
- Highlight matches the community is deliberately avoiding.
- Invite credible supporters from the participating clubs or leagues.
- Remind members to be selective rather than chasing volume.

### Weekend: Matchday

- Share locks as fixtures approach without implying certainty.
- React to settled outcomes.
- Encourage useful discussion around the reasoning, not only the result.
- Capture noteworthy stories for the Monday recap.

### Sunday Evening: Standings

- Publish individual, club, league, and community standings.
- Prepare shareable results for participants.
- Preview the next challenge.

## Signature Campaign

### Which Supporters Know Their League Best?

Run a recurring competition among leagues and clubs based on the collective accuracy of their members.

Examples:

- Premier League vs. Bundesliga
- Serie A vs. La Liga
- Club vs. local rival
- Supporters' group vs. supporters' group
- Podcast audience vs. podcast audience

Each participant makes one selective call. LeagueCred publishes the complete result after settlement. This combines identity, friendly rivalry, transparency, and shareable proof.

## Product-Led Distribution Loop

Every meaningful contribution should create a reason to return or share.

### Required Growth UI

1. **Shareable result cards**
   - Show the member, selection, outcome, lifetime record, and represented league or club.
   - Produce attractive social dimensions for common platforms.
   - Include a LeagueCred link without overwhelming the result.

2. **Personal invitation links**
   - Attribute registrations and activated members to the inviter.
   - Show members the people they successfully brought into the community.

3. **Post-lock invitation prompt**
   - After submitting a lock, ask the member to invite a specialist from another team or league.
   - Keep this optional and dismissible.

4. **Founding badges**
   - Support Founding Member and Founding Captain identities.
   - Keep badges permanent and define how they were earned.

5. **Community challenge pages**
   - Provide club-versus-club, league-versus-league, and partner-community views.
   - Show rules, participants, open locks, settled results, and standings.

6. **Weekly recap pages**
   - Create a permanent, indexable home for each matchweek's results and stories.
   - Link members, leagues, teams, and relevant fixtures.

7. **Public specialist profiles**
   - Show wins, misses, streaks, specialty areas, and recent reasoning.
   - Make selective expertise more prominent than prediction volume.

8. **Representation identity**
   - Let profiles and cards clearly display “Representing [club/league].”

9. **Source attribution**
   - Track invitations, campaigns, partners, and external channels through the full activation funnel.

### Example Share Card

> Serhan called Inter to win — correct<br>
> 7 of 9 lifetime locks<br>
> Representing Serie A on LeagueCred

This is stronger distribution than a generic “Join our website” post because it gives the member identity and credible proof to share.

## Content Distribution Engine

Create one original insight, then adapt it for each suitable channel rather than inventing unrelated content for every network.

One insight can become:

- A TikTok or Instagram Reel
- A YouTube Short
- An X post or short thread
- A Reddit discussion when allowed by community rules
- A shareable visual result card
- A short LeagueCred article or weekly recap
- A discussion prompt inside LeagueCred

### Strong Content Hooks

- “Only 18% of our Premier League specialists trusted this match.”
- “Serie A supporters beat every other league this week.”
- “The match everyone selected—and the specialists avoided.”
- “Three supporters who correctly called four consecutive locks.”
- “What local supporters knew that the general football timeline missed.”
- “This community made fewer calls and achieved better results.”
- “The most confident miss of the week—and what we learned from it.”

### Content Rules

- Use real LeagueCred activity and data.
- Attribute supporter insights to their public profiles.
- Include losses and uncertainty.
- Avoid guaranteed-win language.
- Do not present performance as financial advice.
- Prefer useful, original, first-hand supporter knowledge over mass-produced match previews.
- Use clear authorship and explain how standings or conclusions were calculated.

Google recommends original, trustworthy, people-first material with clear experience and authorship. Public supporter analysis and transparent records can become LeagueCred's long-term search advantage. See [Google's people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).

## Channel Priorities

### Priority 1: Existing Football Communities

Focus on supporter groups, fan forums, Discord servers, subreddits, Facebook groups, podcasts, newsletters, and focused social accounts.

Approach these as collaboration partners. Offer a useful community challenge and transparent recap, not a promotional link drop.

### Priority 2: Member Sharing

Member distribution should center on identity and proof:

- Their settled result
- Their public record
- Their represented club or league
- Their community's current position
- A challenge to a knowledgeable friend or rival

### Priority 3: Short-Form Social Content

Use real weekly stories rather than generic match previews. Create repeatable visual formats so followers recognize LeagueCred immediately.

### Priority 4: Search

Build durable pages around:

- Public specialist profiles
- Team and league communities
- Weekly transparent recaps
- Original supporter explanations
- Challenge and matchweek results

Do not publish hundreds of thin, automated pages solely to target search phrases.

### Priority 5: Lightweight Community Coordination

If Discord is used during the founding phase, keep it deliberately small:

- Welcome
- Weekly locks
- Matchday
- Feedback
- Announcements

Immediately ask each newcomer to introduce their club and league and give them one obvious next action. Discord's community guidance emphasizes recruiting high-intent members and getting newcomers to complete an initial interaction quickly. See [Discord's growth and engagement guidance](https://discord.com/community/using-insights-to-improve-community-growth-engagement).

LeagueCred must remain the primary destination and source of truth. Do not create an external community that makes the product optional.

## 60–90 Day Execution Plan

### Phase 0: Instrumentation and Readiness — Days 1–3

- Finalize the core positioning and free-community message.
- Select the first eight club or league communities.
- Define Founding Member and Founding Captain criteria.
- Create a Founding Captain invitation page.
- Confirm the signup-to-first-lock onboarding path is simple.
- Track acquisition source, signup, selected interests, first lock, return, and invitation.
- Prepare outreach tracking and a list of the first 100 candidates.
- Prepare one reusable challenge format and one transparent recap format.

**Exit criteria:** LeagueCred can identify where a member came from and whether they completed their first meaningful action.

### Phase 1: Establish the First Dense Community — Days 4–10

- Contact 30–40 qualified people per day.
- Recruit the first 10 Founding Captains.
- Activate at least 25 genuine members in one community.
- Run the first Community Lock challenge.
- Personally welcome every new member.
- Ask each activated member what was confusing or missing.

**Exit criteria:** One community has at least 25 members who have made a real lock.

### Phase 2: Prove the Weekly Ritual — Days 11–21

- Run two complete weekly cycles.
- Publish honest recaps with wins and misses.
- Launch supporter spotlights.
- Produce shareable result cards manually if automation is not ready.
- Recruit 20 potential fan-community or creator partners.
- Test two or three club or league rivalries.
- Measure which invitation message and acquisition source produce activated members.

**Exit criteria:** The community returns for a second matchweek and produces stories members willingly share.

### Phase 3: Expand Through Partners — Days 22–45

- Secure 20 small fan-community, podcast, newsletter, or creator collaborations.
- Give each partner a distinct challenge page or tracked source.
- Expand only into communities with a committed captain.
- Publish weekly league-versus-league standings.
- Add invitation and sharing flows to the product.
- Concentrate effort on the top three acquisition sources.

**Exit criteria:** LeagueCred has a repeatable partner playbook and several communities with reliable weekly participation.

### Phase 4: Compound Distribution — Days 46–90

- Scale the best-performing community challenge.
- Turn strong weekly stories into short-form content.
- Publish indexable recap and specialist pages.
- Recognize the most valuable captains and contributors.
- Introduce additional communities only when ownership and initial density are secured.
- Continue personal founder outreach to high-value captains and partners.

**Exit criteria:** 1,000 registered members, at least 300 weekly active lockers, and a measurable invitation/content/partner loop.

## Measurement Framework

### Activation Funnel

1. Visited LeagueCred
2. Created an account
3. Selected a team, league, or region
4. Submitted the first lock
5. Returned the following week
6. Invited another specialist

### Primary Metric

**Weekly active lockers:** unique members who submit a valid lock during the week.

Registrations are a supporting metric. They do not prove that a community exists.

### Initial Targets

- Visitor-to-registration rate: 15% or higher
- Registration-to-first-lock rate: 50% or higher
- First-lock-to-next-week return rate: 35% or higher
- Invitations sent per activated member: 0.5 or higher
- At least 25 weekly active lockers in every launch community
- At least 300 weekly active lockers by the time LeagueCred reaches 1,000 registrations

### Weekly Review

For every acquisition source and community, review:

- Visitors
- Registrations
- First locks
- Activation rate
- Following-week return rate
- Invitations sent
- Referred activated members
- Cost in founder hours
- Qualitative feedback

Double down on sources producing retained lockers, not merely traffic.

## Community Health and Trust

- Publish understandable participation and moderation rules.
- Welcome disagreement about football without permitting harassment.
- Make it easy to report abuse, manipulation, or suspicious records.
- Explain how locks settle and how leaderboards are calculated.
- Clearly distinguish community opinions from guarantees or financial advice.
- Promote responsible, selective participation and provide suitable responsible-gambling guidance.
- Do not use fake scarcity, fake activity, fake users, or hidden sponsored selections.
- Protect the integrity of historical records.

## What Not to Do

- Buy followers, registrations, reviews, or engagement.
- Mass-DM football supporters.
- Spam links across subreddits, groups, or Discord servers.
- Hide losing records.
- Claim that a selection is guaranteed to win.
- Build every league before proving one dense community.
- Offer unrelated giveaways that attract people who do not care about the product.
- Measure success primarily through impressions or registrations.
- Publish mass-produced AI match previews with no original supporter value.
- Let Discord or another social platform become a substitute for LeagueCred.

## Daily Founder Operating System

Until the first 1,000 members:

- **2–3 hours:** personal supporter, captain, and partner conversations
- **1 hour:** create or distribute one original community story
- **30 minutes:** welcome and activate new members
- **30 minutes:** review funnel and retention metrics
- **30 minutes:** collect product feedback and remove activation friction

Personally recruit the first 25 credible supporters in one league and help every one of them make a genuine lock.

That creates the first real LeagueCred community. Everything else—content, referrals, partnerships, league rivalries, and search—can compound from it.
